# Pendum Body support draft

For help with Pendum Body on iPhone or Android, email [monitored support email]. Include the app version, operating-system version, phone model and a short description of the issue. Please avoid sending private health records or photos unless necessary.

## Your local record

The app works without an account. It stores weight, height, dated body measurements, measured body fat, the selected body-fat equation, preferences and progress photos on your phone. There is no in-app backup or export. Deleting the app may remove these records; device backups depend on your settings. We cannot restore records from a developer server.

## Health sync

Enable health sync in Settings and grant the desired permissions. On iOS, weight and height sync both ways, and recorded body-fat percentages and waist circumference are exported to Apple Health. On Android, weight and height sync both ways, and recorded body-fat percentages are exported to Health Connect. Photos, calculated body-fat estimates, BMI, FFMI and other tape measurements are not exported. Imported records are managed by their original source; deleting them here hides the local copy. Deleting app-origin measurements queues linked health-record deletion for the next successful sync. Turning sync off stops future sync but leaves previously exported records in the health service. Background timing depends on your device; open the app to let pending work retry.

## Settings and corrections

Use Settings to choose units, language and appearance. Edit saved measurement entries from their history list. Open a photo to change its date or pose or delete it. Records imported from Health are managed by their source. Trend weight smooths daily average readings, so it may differ from the latest scale reading.
