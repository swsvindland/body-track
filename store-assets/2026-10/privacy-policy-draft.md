Pendum Body stores weight, height, dated body measurements, measured body fat, the selected body-fat equation, preferences and progress photos in local app storage. You can use it without an account. The reviewed implementation has no advertising, analytics or developer-hosted record service. We do not receive your tracking records through the app.

## Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access when you take a photo. You can also select photos using the system picker. Selected images are copied into the app's private document storage. They are not uploaded to a developer server or exported to a health service. You can delete saved photos in the app.

## Optional health integration

Health sync is off until you enable it and grant permissions. On iOS, weight and height sync both ways, and recorded body-fat percentages and waist circumference are exported to Apple Health. On Android, weight and height sync both ways, and recorded body-fat percentages are exported to Health Connect. Photos, calculated body-fat estimates, BMI, FFMI and other tape measurements are not exported. Imported records are managed by their original source; deleting them here hides the local copy. Deleting app-origin measurements queues linked health-record deletion for the next successful sync.

Disable sync in Settings to stop future integration, and manage individual permissions in Apple Health or Health Connect. Disconnecting does not remove records already saved to the health service. Those services, operating-system backups and other apps you authorize follow their own privacy practices. Background processing is controlled by the operating system and may be delayed.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them or app storage is removed. The app offers no in-app cloud backup or data export. Deleting the app may delete its local records; it does not remove prior health exports. Device backups may contain app data depending on your settings. Manage those backups with your operating system or backup provider. Delete records inside the app while health sync is enabled and let sync complete if you also want the app to remove linked app-origin health exports. Health records can also be managed in the health service.
