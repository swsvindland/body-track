## Apple App Store

### Name

Pendum Body: Weight Tracker

### Subtitle

Measurements & progress photos

### Promotional Text

See your progress beyond the scale. Track weight trends, 16 body measurements and private progress photos, with optional Apple Health sync.

### Keywords

bmi,fat,ffmi,waist,scale,tape,composition,physique,journal,stone,lean,log,height

### Description

Your progress is more than a number on the scale.

Pendum Body brings weight trends, body measurements and progress photos together in a personal record on your phone. Follow changes over time, without creating an account.

SEE THE DIRECTION
Log your weight and follow a smoothed 90-day trend alongside your daily readings. Add, edit and backdate entries to keep your history accurate.

MEASURE MORE OF THE PICTURE
Record 16 optional tape measurements, including waist, shoulders, chest, hips and left and right limbs. Add height and a measured body-fat percentage, or use supported circumference measurements for an estimate. View BMI, FFMI and shoulder-to-waist ratio with explanations and reference context.

KEEP A VISUAL RECORD
Save front, side and back photos in a dated gallery. Compare two photos of the same pose, open an image for a closer look, or update its date and pose. Take a photo or choose one from your library. Saved photos stay in the app's local storage.

CONNECT WITH APPLE HEALTH
Choose to sync weight and height in both directions. Save recorded body-fat percentages and waist circumference to Apple Health. Other tape measurements, photos and calculated estimates stay in Pendum Body. Health sync requires your permission; background timing depends on iOS.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose kilograms and centimeters, pounds and inches, or decimal stone and inches. Use light, dark or system appearance. Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

ONE PURCHASE. YOUR OWN RECORD.
A paid download with no subscription, ads or sign-in. Core tracking works offline. The app does not provide its own cloud backup or export. Deleting the app may remove local records and photos; device backups depend on your settings.

Pendum Body is a fitness record, not a medical device. BMI, estimated body fat and FFMI are general adult reference tools. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
