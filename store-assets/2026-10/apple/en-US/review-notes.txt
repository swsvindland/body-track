Pendum Body is a paid-download Health & Fitness app with no sign-in, subscription or in-app purchases. No review account is needed; all core screens are accessible after installing the app. Health sync and photo permissions are optional.

1. Open Overview and add a weight entry. Add height from the Height tab, then return to Overview.
2. Open Body and add a session. Sixteen circumference sites are optional. A recorded body-fat percentage takes precedence over an estimate. Male estimates require neck, abdomen and height; female estimates require neck, waist, hips and height. Select the equation in Settings.
3. Open Photos, add a photo with a date and pose, then open it to edit. Select two photos of the same pose to compare.
4. Try units, language and theme in Settings.
5. Enable health sync and grant the permissions you want to test. Denying health or photo access does not block manual weight/measurement logging.

On iOS, weight and height sync both ways, and recorded body-fat percentages and waist circumference are exported to Apple Health.



BMI, estimated body fat and FFMI are adult reference tools, not diagnostic results. The app is not a medical device. Background execution depends on the operating system.

Store screenshots use fictional demonstration records. Any sample progress photos are illustrative and do not promise a transformation.
